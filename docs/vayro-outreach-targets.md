## Outreach rules

1. Start with the official application/contact path; use a public business inbox only when the organization publishes one.
2. Personalize the first paragraph to the target’s actual thesis or partner program.
3. Ask for a 15-minute fit conversation, not a commitment.
4. Do not claim live bookings, revenue, customers, insurance coverage, or verified traction unless those facts are documented.
5. For insurance, ask about licensing, states served, underwriting appetite, claims handling, and whether the product can cover cars, boats, RVs, trailers, motorcycles, ATVs, and UTVs.
6. For hotel/destination partners, propose a tracked member benefit or affiliate arrangement—not an implied guaranteed discount.
